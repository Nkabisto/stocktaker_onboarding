4. **Document your decisions:**
   - Why did you choose pre-generation vs on-demand?
   Pre-generation is better for performance as I'm not generating queries every time a user loads the page.
   It offers admin greater control as they can manually deactivate specific dates and times.
   
   - Why did you choose pessimistic vs optimistic locking?
   I choose optimistic locking because it's more efficient as people are choosing between different days/times so the probability of multiple people 
   selecting the same days/time slots at the same time is low, as the number of expected daily visitors is less than a thousand.
